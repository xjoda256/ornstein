CALLING ALL COOKS
A Southern-Mexican Cookbook


-- APPETIZERS AND DIPS --

DEVILED EGGS
- 6 hard-boiled eggs
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp lime juice
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tbsp cilantro, chopped
- Salt, pepper
- Chili powder

Slice eggs in half. Scoop yolks. Mash with mayo, mustard, lime juice, jalapeño, and cilantro. Pipe into whites. Dust with chili powder.

QUESO DIP
- 1 lb Velveeta cheese
- 1 can Ro-Tel tomatoes
- 1 lb ground beef, browned
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1 tsp cumin

Melt cheese. Add tomatoes, beef, onion, jalapeños, and cumin. Serve warm with chips.

GUACAMOLE
- 3 avocados, mashed
- 1/2 cup onion, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt

Mix all ingredients.

PICO DE GALLO
- 4 Roma tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 2 limes, juiced
- Salt

Mix all ingredients.

SALSA VERDE
- 1 lb tomatillos, husked
- 3 jalapeños
- 1/2 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup cilantro
- Salt

Boil tomatillos and jalapeños until soft. Blend with onion, garlic, cilantro, and salt.

PIMENTO CHEESE
- 2 cups shredded cheddar
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 jar pimentos, drained
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Mix all ingredients. Serve on crackers, bread, or with celery.

NACHOS
- 1 bag tortilla chips
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 1 can black beans
- 1 jalapeño, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 serrano pepper, sliced
- Sour cream, salsa

Layer chips, cheese, beans, jalapeño, serrano, and pepper. Bake until cheese melts. Top with tomato, sour cream, and salsa.

FRIED PICKLES
- 1 jar dill pickle chips
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt, pepper, cayenne
- Oil for frying

Drain pickles. Dip in buttermilk, then in seasoned flour/cornmeal mixture with cumin and chili powder. Fry in hot oil until golden.

ELOTE IN A CUP
- 4 cups corn, charred
- 1/2 cup mayo
- 1 cup cotija cheese
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- Lime juice
- Cilantro

Toss everything together.


-- SOUTHERN FAVORITES --

FRIED GREEN TOMATOES
- 4 green tomatoes, sliced
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Oil for frying

Dip tomatoes in egg, coat in seasoned cornmeal with cumin and chili powder, fry in hot oil 2-3 min per side.

BISCUITS AND GRAVY
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup shortening
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 lb sausage
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 cups milk
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Mix biscuit ingredients. Roll out, cut, bake 450°F for 10-12 min. Brown sausage. Add flour, adobo sauce, and cumin, stir. Slowly add milk, stir until thick. Pour over biscuits.

HUSH PUPPIES
- 2 cups cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup onion, minced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- Oil for frying

Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients. Combine. Stir in jalapeños. Drop by spoonfuls into hot oil. Fry until golden brown.

CORNBREAD
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 2 jalapeños, diced
- 1 cup shredded cheddar

Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients. Combine. Stir in jalapeños and cheese. Pour into greased skillet. Bake 400°F for 20-25 min.

COLLARD GREENS
- 2 bunches collard greens, stems removed and chopped
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp vinegar
- Salt, pepper, cumin

Sauté onion, garlic, and chipotles. Add broth, adobo sauce, and greens. Simmer 1-2 hours until tender. Add sugar, vinegar, and cumin.

SMOTHERED OKRA
- 4 cups fresh okra, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1/4 cup bacon grease or oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Sauté okra, onion, bell pepper, garlic, and jalapeño in bacon grease until browned. Add tomatoes and cumin. Simmer 20-30 min until tender.

RED BEANS AND RICE
- 2 cups dried red beans, soaked overnight
- 1 lb sausage, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp cayenne
- Salt, pepper

Cook beans with sausage, onion, pepper, garlic, jalapeños, and seasonings until tender. Serve over rice.

GRITS
- 1 cup quick grits
- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup shredded pepper jack
- 1 jalapeño, minced

Boil water and salt. Stir in grits. Cook 5 min. Stir in butter, cheese, and jalapeño.

SWEET TEA
- 3 family size tea bags
- 1 cup sugar
- 8 cups water
- 1 lime, sliced

Boil 4 cups water. Steep tea bags 5 min. Remove bags, stir in sugar. Add remaining water and lime slices. Chill.


-- CASSEROLES --

MAC AND CHEESE
- 8 oz elbow macaroni
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 3 cups milk
- 4 cups shredded pepper jack
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Cook macaroni. Melt butter, whisk in flour, slowly add milk, stir until thick. Add cheese, chipotles, adobo, and cumin. Mix with macaroni. Pour into greased dish. Bake 350°F for 30 min.

CHIPOTLE MAC AND CHEESE
- 8 oz elbow macaroni

- 3 cups milk
- 4 cups shredded pepper jack
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced

- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 cup corn
- Salt, pepper, cumin

Cook macaroni. Melt butter, whisk in flour, slowly add milk, stir until thick. Add cheese, chipotles, and adobo sauce. Mix with macaroni, beans, and corn. Pour into greased dish. Bake 350°F for 30 min.

-- SOUPS --

MEXICAN STREET CORN SOUP
- 4 cups frozen corn
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup cotija cheese
- 2 jalapeños, diced
- 1 tsp cumin
- Chili powder, lime juice

Sauté onion, garlic, and jalapeños. Add corn, broth, and cumin. Simmer 15 min. Stir in sour cream and cheese. Top with chili powder and lime.

HOT AND SOUR SOUP
- 8 cups vegetable broth
- 1 block firm tofu, cubed
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 1/2 cup bamboo shoots
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 green onion, chopped
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced

Bring broth to boil. Add tofu, mushrooms, bamboo shoots, bell pepper, and jalapeño. Simmer 5 min. Add soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, and cumin. Top with green onion.

EGG DROP SOUP
- 8 cups vegetable broth
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 1 green onion, chopped
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced

Bring broth to boil. Add bell pepper and jalapeño. Slowly drizzle eggs while stirring. Add sesame oil and green onion.

-- DESSERTS AND DRINKS --

PEACH COBBLER
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 4 cups peaches, sliced
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of cayenne

Melt butter in baking dish. Mix flour, sugar, milk, and baking powder. Pour over butter. Add peaches on top. Sprinkle with cinnamon and cayenne. Bake 350°F for 45-50 min.

BANANA PUDDING
- 1 box vanilla wafers
- 6 bananas, sliced
- 2 cups milk
- 1 box instant vanilla pudding
- 8 oz whipped topping
- Pinch of cinnamon

Make pudding with milk. Layer wafers, bananas, and pudding in dish. Dust with cinnamon. Top with whipped topping. Chill 4 hours.

MEXICAN HOT CHOCOLATE
- 4 cups milk
- 1/2 cup cocoa powder
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/4 tsp chili powder

Heat milk. Whisk in cocoa, sugar, cinnamon, cayenne, and chili powder. Serve hot.

WATERMELON AGUA FRESCA
- 8 cups watermelon, cubed
- 4 cups water
- 1/2 cup lime juice
- 1/4 cup sugar
- Fresh mint
- 2 jalapeños, sliced

Blend watermelon, strain. Mix with water, lime juice, sugar, and jalapeños. Serve cold.
