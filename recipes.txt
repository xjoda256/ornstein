FRIED GREEN TOMATOES
- 4 green tomatoes, sliced
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Oil for frying

1. Heat 1/4 inch of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers.
2. Mix cornmeal with cumin, chili powder, salt, pepper, and garlic powder on a plate.
3. Dip each tomato slice in beaten egg, letting excess drip off.
4. Press into the seasoned cornmeal, coating both sides evenly.
5. Fry 2-3 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy.
6. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate. Serve immediately.

MAC AND CHEESE
- 8 oz elbow macaroni
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 3 cups milk
- 4 cups shredded pepper jack
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Cook macaroni in salted boiling water until al dente. Drain and set aside.
3. In a large saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook 1 minute.
4. Slowly pour in milk, whisking constantly to avoid lumps.
5. Cook over medium heat, stirring often, until the sauce thickens and coats the back of a spoon (about 5 minutes).
6. Remove from heat. Stir in pepper jack, chipotles, adobo sauce, and cumin until cheese is melted and smooth.
7. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Fold in the cooked macaroni.
8. Pour into the prepared baking dish. Bake 30 minutes until bubbly and lightly golden on top.

HUSH PUPPIES
- 2 cups cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup onion, minced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- Oil for frying

1. Heat 2-3 inches of oil in a deep pot to 375°F.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together cornmeal, flour, baking powder, and salt.
3. In a separate bowl, combine eggs, buttermilk, and onion. Pour into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
4. Fold in jalapeños.
5. Drop batter by spoonfuls into the hot oil, being careful not to crowd the pot.
6. Fry 3-4 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown all over.
7. Remove with a slotted spoon to a paper towel-lined plate. Serve hot.

BISCUITS AND GRAVY
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup shortening
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 lb sausage
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 cups milk
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. For the biscuits: combine 2 cups flour, baking powder, and salt. Cut in shortening with a fork or pastry cutter until crumbly.
3. Stir in milk until a soft dough forms. Turn out onto a floured surface and roll to 1/2-inch thickness. Cut with a biscuit cutter.
4. Place on a baking sheet and bake 10-12 minutes until golden.
5. For the gravy: in a skillet over medium heat, brown sausage, breaking it into crumbles, about 6-7 minutes.
6. Sprinkle 1/4 cup flour over the sausage and stir 1 minute. Add adobo sauce and cumin.
7. Slowly pour in milk, stirring constantly. Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
8. Split biscuits and pour gravy over the top.

COLLARD GREENS
- 2 bunches collard greens, stems removed and chopped
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp vinegar
- Salt, pepper, cumin

1. In a large pot, sauté onion and garlic over medium heat until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Add chipotle peppers and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Pour in chicken broth and adobo sauce. Bring to a boil.
4. Add collard greens in batches, stirring to wilt them down.
5. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer 1-2 hours until greens are very tender.
6. Stir in sugar, vinegar, and cumin. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

DEVILED EGGS
- 6 hard-boiled eggs
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp lime juice
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tbsp cilantro, chopped
- Salt, pepper
- Chili powder

1. Slice eggs in half lengthwise. Gently remove yolks to a bowl.
2. Mash yolks with a fork until smooth. Add mayonnaise, mustard, and lime juice. Mix until creamy.
3. Fold in minced jalapeño and cilantro. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Spoon or pipe the yolk mixture back into the egg white halves.
5. Dust with chili powder. Chill until serving.

REFRIED BEANS
- 2 cans pinto beans
- 1/4 cup lard or oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

1. In a skillet, heat lard or oil over medium heat. Sauté onion, garlic, and jalapeño until softened, about 5 minutes.

5. Season with cumin, salt, and pepper. Serve warm.

COLESLAW

PEACH COBBLER
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tsp baking powder

- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of cayenne

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.

SWEET TEA
- 3 family size tea bags
- 1 cup sugar
- 8 cups water
- 1 lime, sliced

1. Bring 4 cups of water to a boil in a saucepan.
2. Remove from heat. Add tea bags and steep 5 minutes. Do not squeeze the bags.

SPOONBREAD
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 3 eggs, separated
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 jalapeño, minced

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 1-quart baking dish.
2. In a saucepan, cook cornmeal with water over medium heat, stirring constantly, until very thick.
3. Remove from heat. Stir in milk, melted butter, egg yolks, baking powder, and jalapeño.
4. In a clean bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form.
5. Gently fold the egg whites into the cornmeal mixture.
6. Pour into the prepared baking dish. Bake 35-40 minutes until puffed and golden.

CHILE RELLENOS
- 4 poblano peppers
- 2 cups shredded Monterey Jack
- 4 eggs, separated
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp cumin
- Oil for frying

1. Roast poblanos over an open flame or under the broiler, turning frequently, until charred all over.
2. Place in a bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let steam 10 minutes. Peel off skin, cut a slit lengthwise, and carefully remove seeds.
3. Stuff each pepper with shredded Monterey Jack. Close the slit.
4. In a bowl, beat egg whites until stiff peaks form. Gently fold in egg yolks and cumin.
5. Heat 1/2 inch of oil in a skillet over medium heat.
6. Dredge each stuffed pepper in flour, then dip in the egg batter.
7. Fry until golden on all sides, about 2-3 minutes per side. Serve immediately.
