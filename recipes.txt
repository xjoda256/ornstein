A Southern-Mexican-Chinese Cookbook

-- APPETIZERS AND DIPS --

DEVILED EGGS
- 6 hard-boiled eggs
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 tsp lime juice
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tbsp cilantro, chopped
- Salt, pepper
- Chili powder

Slice eggs in half. Scoop yolks. Mash with mayo, mustard, lime juice, jalapeño, and cilantro. Pipe into whites. Dust with chili powder.

GUACAMOLE
- 3 avocados, mashed
- 1/2 cup onion, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 2 limes, juiced
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt

Mix all ingredients.

PICO DE GALLO
- 4 Roma tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- 2 limes, juiced
- Salt

Mix all ingredients.

SALSA VERDE
- 1 lb tomatillos, husked
- 3 jalapeños
- 1/2 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/4 cup cilantro
- Salt

Boil tomatillos and jalapeños until soft. Blend with onion, garlic, cilantro, and salt.

PIMENTO CHEESE
- 2 cups shredded cheddar
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 jar pimentos, drained
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Mix all ingredients. Serve on crackers, bread, or with celery.

NACHOS
- 1 bag tortilla chips
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 1 can black beans
- 1 jalapeño, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 serrano pepper, sliced
- Sour cream, salsa

Layer chips, cheese, beans, jalapeño, serrano, and pepper. Bake until cheese melts. Top with tomato, sour cream, and salsa.

FRIED PICKLES
- 1 jar dill pickle chips
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt, pepper, cayenne
- Oil for frying

Drain pickles. Dip in buttermilk, then in seasoned flour/cornmeal mixture with cumin and chili powder. Fry in hot oil until golden.

ELOTE IN A CUP
- 4 cups corn, charred
- 1/2 cup mayo
- 1 cup cotija cheese
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- Lime juice
- Cilantro

Toss everything together.

-- SOUTHERN FAVORITES --

FRIED GREEN TOMATOES
- 4 green tomatoes, sliced
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Oil for frying

Dip tomatoes in egg, coat in seasoned cornmeal with cumin and chili powder, fry in hot oil 2-3 min per side.

CORNBREAD
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 2 jalapeños, diced
- 1 cup shredded cheddar

Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients. Combine. Stir in jalapeños and cheese. Pour into greased skillet. Bake 400°F for 20-25 min.

HUSH PUPPIES
- 2 cups cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup onion, minced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- Oil for frying

Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients. Combine. Stir in jalapeños. Drop by spoonfuls into hot oil. Fry until golden brown.

GRITS
- 1 cup quick grits
- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup shredded pepper jack
- 1 jalapeño, minced

Boil water and salt. Stir in grits. Cook 5 min. Stir in butter, cheese, and jalapeño.

SWEET TEA
- 3 family size tea bags
- 1 cup sugar
- 8 cups water
- 1 lime, sliced

Boil 4 cups water. Steep tea bags 5 min. Remove bags, stir in sugar. Add remaining water and lime slices. Chill.

BISCUITS AND GRAVY
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/3 cup shortening
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 lb vegetarian sausage
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 cups milk
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Mix biscuit ingredients. Roll out, cut, bake 450°F for 10-12 min. Brown vegetarian sausage. Add flour, adobo sauce, and cumin, stir. Slowly add milk, stir until thick. Pour over biscuits.

-- CASSEROLES --

MAC AND CHEESE
- 8 oz elbow macaroni
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 3 cups milk
- 4 cups shredded pepper jack
- 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Cook macaroni. Melt butter, whisk in flour, slowly add milk, stir until thick. Add cheese, chipotles, adobo, and cumin. Mix with macaroni. Pour into greased dish. Bake 350°F for 30 min.

SQUASH CASSEROLE
- 4 cups yellow squash, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño, diced
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup shredded cheddar
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- Salt, pepper, cumin

Cook squash, onion, and jalapeño until soft. Mix with eggs, cheese, and breadcrumbs. Pour into greased dish. Drizzle with butter. Bake 350°F for 30 min.

GREEN BEAN CASSEROLE
- 4 cups green beans, cooked
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup french fried onions
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Mix green beans, soup, bell pepper, and jalapeño. Pour into greased dish. Top with fried onions. Bake 350°F for 25 min.

CORN CASSEROLE
- 1 can whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 can cream style corn
- 1 box Jiffy corn muffin mix
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 cup shredded pepper jack

Mix all ingredients except half the cheese. Pour into greased dish. Top with remaining cheese. Bake 350°F for 45-50 min.

STUFFED GREEN PEPPERS
- 4 green peppers, tops cut off and seeded
- 1 cup cooked rice
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 jalapeños, diced
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder
- 1 cup shredded cheese

Mix rice, beans, half the tomato sauce, onion, jalapeños, and seasonings. Stuff peppers. Pour remaining sauce over top. Top with cheese. Bake 350°F for 30-35 min.

BROCCOLI AND RICE CASSEROLE
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 2 cups chopped broccoli
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup shredded cheddar
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 serrano pepper, minced
- Salt, pepper, cumin

Mix all ingredients. Pour into greased dish. Bake 350°F for 30 min.

CHEESE GRITS CASSEROLE
- 3 cups cooked grits
- 2 cups shredded pepper jack
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 cup milk
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Mix all ingredients. Pour into greased dish. Bake 350°F for 45-50 min.

CORN PUDDING
- 2 cans whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 can cream style corn
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 box Jiffy corn muffin mix
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 cup shredded pepper jack

Mix all ingredients except half the cheese. Pour into greased dish. Top with remaining cheese. Bake 350°F for 45-50 min.

-- SOUPS --

MEXICAN STREET CORN SOUP
- 4 cups frozen corn
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup cotija cheese
- 2 jalapeños, diced
- 1 tsp cumin
- Chili powder, lime juice

Sauté onion, garlic, and jalapeños. Add corn, broth, and cumin. Simmer 15 min. Stir in sour cream and cheese. Top with chili powder and lime.

HOT AND SOUR SOUP
- 8 cups vegetable broth
- 1 block firm tofu, cubed
- 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
- 1/2 cup bamboo shoots
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 green onion, chopped
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced

Bring broth to boil. Add tofu, mushrooms, bamboo shoots, bell pepper, and jalapeño. Simmer 5 min. Add soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, and cumin. Top with green onion.

EGG DROP SOUP
- 8 cups vegetable broth
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 1 green onion, chopped
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced

Bring broth to boil. Add bell pepper and jalapeño. Slowly drizzle eggs while stirring. Add sesame oil and green onion.

WONTON SOUP
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 package wonton wrappers
- 1 cup cabbage, shredded
- 1/2 cup carrots, shredded
- 2 green onions, chopped
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced

Mix cabbage, carrots, green onions, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic. Place 1 tbsp filling in each wonton wrapper, fold into triangles, seal. Bring broth to boil. Drop in wontons and cook 5 min until they float.

-- MEXICAN AND TEX-MEX --

MEXICAN RICE
- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- Salt

Sauté rice until golden. Add vegetables, garlic, jalapeño, and seasonings. Add broth and tomato sauce. Simmer until rice is tender.

MEXICAN CORNBREAD
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 cup shredded cheddar
- 3 jalapeños, diced
- 1 can creamed corn

Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients. Combine. Add cheese, jalapeños, and corn. Bake 400°F for 25-30 min.

JALAPEÑO CORNBREAD WAFFLES
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 3 jalapeños, diced
- 1 cup shredded cheddar

Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients. Combine. Stir in jalapeños and cheese. Pour into waffle iron. Cook until golden.

ENCHILADAS
- 8 corn tortillas
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 2 cans enchilada sauce
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 jalapeños, diced

Fill tortillas with cheese, onion, pepper, and jalapeños. Roll up. Pour sauce over top. Bake 350°F for 20 min.

BEAN AND CHEESE QUESADILLAS
- 4 flour tortillas
- 1 can refried beans
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 3 jalapeños, diced
- 1 tsp cumin

Spread beans on tortillas. Top with cheese, onion, pepper, jalapeños, and cumin. Top with second tortilla. Cook until golden.

QUICK CHILAQUILES
- 1 bag tortilla chips
- 2 cups salsa verde
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 4 jalapeños, sliced
- 4 eggs (optional)

Heat chips in a pan. Toss with salsa. Top with cheese, sour cream, jalapeños, and fried eggs.

CHILE RELLENOS
- 4 poblano peppers
- 2 cups shredded Monterey Jack
- 4 eggs, separated
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp cumin
- Oil for frying

Roast and peel peppers. Stuff with cheese. Beat egg whites until stiff, fold in yolks and cumin. Dip peppers in batter, fry until golden.

HUEVOS RANCHEROS
- 4 eggs
- 1 can black beans
- 1 cup salsa
- 4 corn tortillas
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 jalapeños, sliced

Fry eggs. Warm beans and salsa. Warm tortillas. Top with beans, eggs, salsa, cheese, avocado, and jalapeños.

QUESO DIP
- 1 lb Velveeta cheese
- 1 can Ro-Tel tomatoes
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1 tsp cumin

Melt cheese. Add tomatoes, onion, jalapeños, and cumin. Serve warm with chips.

REFRIED BEANS
- 2 cans pinto beans
- 1/4 cup lard or oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Sauté onion, garlic, and jalapeño in lard. Add beans, mash and cook until thick. Season with cumin, salt, and pepper.

MEXICAN FRIED RICE
- 4 cups cooked rice
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup corn
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp cumin
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup green onions, chopped

Scramble eggs, set aside. Sauté garlic, jalapeño, and bell pepper. Add rice, corn, beans, and seasonings. Stir-fry. Add soy sauce. Top with eggs and green onions.

MEXICAN QUINOA BOWL
- 1 cup quinoa, cooked

- 1 cup salsa
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 bell pepper, diced

Mix all ingredients. Serve warm or cold.

ENCHILADA SAUCE
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 can tomato sauce
- Salt

Whisk all ingredients together. Simmer 10 min until thick.

- 2 jalapeños, minced
- 1 cup cilantro, chopped
- 1 tsp cumin

Toss everything together. Chill before serving.

POTATO SALAD
- 6 potatoes, boiled and cubed
- 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp mustard
- 1 cup celery, diced
- 1/2 cup onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- Salt, pepper, cumin

Mix all ingredients. Chill before serving.

BLACK-EYED PEA SALAD
- 2 cans black-eyed peas, drained
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 cup Italian dressing
- 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped
- Salt, pepper, cumin

Mix all ingredients. Chill overnight.

SMOTHERED OKRA
- 4 cups fresh okra, sliced
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1/4 cup bacon grease or oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt, pepper

Sauté okra, onion, bell pepper, garlic, and jalapeño in bacon grease until browned. Add tomatoes and cumin. Simmer 20-30 min until tender.

SPOONBREAD
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup water
- 3 eggs, separated
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 jalapeño, minced

Cook cornmeal with water until thick. Stir in milk, butter, egg yolks, baking powder, and jalapeño. Beat egg whites until stiff, fold in. Bake 350°F for 35-40 min.

-- PASTA --

BAKED ZITI
- 1 lb ziti pasta
- 1 jar marinara sauce
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella
- 1 cup ricotta cheese
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1 tsp basil
- Salt, pepper

Cook ziti. Sauté onion, garlic, and jalapeño. Add marinara sauce. Mix with ziti, ricotta, half the mozzarella, and parmesan. Pour into baking dish. Top with remaining mozzarella. Bake 375°F for 25 min.

SPAGHETTI WITH MARINARA
- 1 lb spaghetti
- 1 jar marinara sauce
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 1/4 cup parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp basil
- 1 tsp oregano
- Salt, pepper
- Olive oil

Cook spaghetti. Sauté onion, garlic, and jalapeño in olive oil. Add marinara, basil, and oregano. Simmer 15 min. Toss with spaghetti. Top with parmesan.

FETTUCCINE ALFREDO
- 1 lb fettuccine
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 cups parmesan cheese, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- Salt, pepper
- Parsley

Cook fettuccine. Melt butter, add garlic and jalapeño, cook 1 min. Add cream, simmer 3 min. Stir in parmesan until melted. Toss with fettuccine. Season with salt and pepper. Top with parsley.

PASTA PRIMAVERA
- 1 lb penne
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 cup carrots, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup snap peas
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 jalapeño, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp basil
- Salt, pepper

Cook penne. Sauté broccoli, carrots, bell pepper, snap peas, garlic, and jalapeño in olive oil until crisp-tender. Toss with pasta and parmesan. Season with basil, salt, and pepper.

MACARONI AND CHEESE (BAKED)
- 1 lb elbow macaroni
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 3 cups milk
- 3 cups shredded cheddar
- 1 cup shredded pepper jack
- 1 tsp mustard powder
- Salt, pepper
- 1 cup breadcrumbs

Cook macaroni. Melt butter, whisk in flour, slowly add milk, stir until thick. Add cheeses and mustard powder. Mix with macaroni. Pour into baking dish. Top with breadcrumbs. Bake 350°F for 30 min.

-- DESSERTS AND DRINKS --

PEACH COBBLER
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 4 cups peaches, sliced
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- Pinch of cayenne

Melt butter in baking dish. Mix flour, sugar, milk, and baking powder. Pour over butter. Add peaches on top. Sprinkle with cinnamon and cayenne. Bake 350°F for 45-50 min.

BANANA PUDDING
- 1 box vanilla wafers
- 6 bananas, sliced
- 2 cups milk
- 1 box instant vanilla pudding
- 8 oz whipped topping
- Pinch of cinnamon

Make pudding with milk. Layer wafers, bananas, and pudding in dish. Dust with cinnamon. Top with whipped topping. Chill 4 hours.

MEXICAN HOT CHOCOLATE

- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/4 tsp chili powder

Heat milk. Whisk in cocoa, sugar, cinnamon, cayenne, and chili powder. Serve hot.

WATERMELON AGUA FRESCA

Blend watermelon, strain. Mix with water, lime juice, sugar, and jalapeños. Serve cold.
